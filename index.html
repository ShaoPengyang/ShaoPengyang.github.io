About

I am a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at the National University of Singapore, working with Prof. Tat-Seng Chua. I received my Ph.D. from Hefei University of Technology in July 2025, under the supervision of Prof. Meng Wang.

My research focuses on trustworthy AI and AI + X. I currently study LLM unlearning and safety, recommender systems, and intelligent education, with a broader interest in making AI systems reliable, safe, and responsible.

Open for research interns

I welcome self-motivated students interested in trustworthy AI, LLM unlearning, AI safety, and AI + X. Please email a short introduction, your CV, and your research interests.
