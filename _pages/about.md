Pengyang Shao received his Ph.D. degree in July 2025 from Hefei University of Technology under the supervision of Prof. Meng Wang. He is currently at NExT++ Lab, National University of Singapore, focusing on LLM mechanisms, including unlearning, personalization, and agents.  


---

Research Interests
======
- LLM unlearning
- Agent safety
- LLM applications
