  <div class="profile-heading">
    <img src="/images/profile.png" alt="Shao Pengyang">
    <div><h1>Shao Pengyang <span>(邵鹏阳)</span></h1><p>Postdoctoral Research Fellow at National University of Singapore</p></div>
  </div>
  <p>Hi, I am <strong>Shao Pengyang</strong>, currently a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at the <strong>National University of Singapore</strong>, working with Prof. <a href="https://www.comp.nus.edu.sg/cs/people/chuats/">Tat-Seng Chua</a>. Before joining NUS, I received my Ph.D. from <strong>Hefei University of Technology</strong>, advised by Prof. <a href="https://sites.google.com/view/meng-wang/home">Meng Wang</a>.</p>
  <p>My research focuses on <strong>trustworthy AI</strong> and <strong>AI + X</strong>. I currently study LLM unlearning and safety, recommender systems, and intelligent education, with a broader interest in developing AI systems that are reliable, safe, and responsible in real-world applications.</p>
  <p class="intern-line"><strong>I am open to research interns and self-motivated students interested in trustworthy AI, LLM unlearning, AI safety, and AI + X.</strong> Please send a short introduction, your CV, and research interests by email.</p>
